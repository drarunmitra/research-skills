# Document types and section order

The shared spine is verified across the guides: problem, options, implementation. The order and page counts beyond that are house style. Page budgets are untested.

Rule for all types: sections 1 to 3 of a brief fit on page 1. The reader can decide from page 1 alone.

## A. Policy brief (Minister, Secretary, Collector, DM&HO)

Length: 2 to 4 pages plus annex. House style.

1. Decision requested, by whom, by when, with cost.
2. Recommendation, labelled as the author's opinion. One strongest reason. One "least certain part" line.
3. Key messages: 4 to 6 lines on what the findings mean for this reader.
4. Problem: size, who, where, trend. Local number first.
5. Options (status quo plus two or three): benefits, harms, cost, certainty. Use the compact option table in `frameworks.md`.
6. Cost and who pays: yearly cost, budget head, low, central, high, basis label.
7. Fit for this place: equity, acceptability, feasibility, does the evidence transfer.
8. Implementation: owner, first steps with dates, main barriers and fixes, who must agree.
9. Limits (at most five), signposts, triggers, review date.
10. Draft order or resolution text with sign-off block. Marked "draft, not issued".
11. Annex: sources, methods, certainty ratings, authors (institution), funder, conflicts, reviewers, open-items list.

## B. Action plan (Collector, DM&HO, Mission Director)

1. Decision and approval requested.
2. Objective, baseline and target, in plain numbers with source.
3. Half-page results chain: activity, output, outcome, key assumption.
4. Activity table: what, who, by when, dependency, cost.
5. Budget: line items, per year, source head, gap, not-costed list, contingency.
6. Risks and barriers (four or five), each with fix and owner.
7. Monitoring: indicator, data source, frequency, reviewer, trigger for changing course. Frame on reach, adoption, implementation, maintenance.
8. Governance: review meetings and escalation path.
9. Limits and assumptions, each with a mark asking the officer to confirm.
10. Annex: evidence summary, assumptions, data sources, district lists.

## C. Dossier (reference file for a decision cycle)

1. Cover note with the decision ask (brief sections 1 to 3).
2. Contents and reader's guide: which section for whom.
3. Executive summary (about three pages).
4. Situation analysis: problem, local data, stakeholders, context.
5. Options appraisal: one option table per option, equity notes.
6. Evidence summaries: design, setting, certainty for each source. State the search method.
7. Costing: budget impact with input table. Add CHEERS-style reporting only if the dossier includes an economic evaluation.
8. Implementation plan (action plan structure) and monitoring.
9. Consultation record: stakeholders, MPs, MLAs and PRIs for district plans.
10. Draft orders, letters, templates.
11. Methods, limits, funding, conflicts, reviewers, version log, to-verify register.

## D. Cabinet note (central style; state format not verified)

Source: Cabinet Secretariat handbook, read in full. Check the state format against a recent note.

Rules: A4; numbered paragraphs, no bullets; main note about 7 to 8 pages double spaced; tables and maps in annexes; acronyms expanded; last paragraph records the Minister's approval.

Order: subject; introduction of 3 to 4 sentences with the essence and the ask; background; proposal; justification and evidence with certainty and basis labels; options considered; appraisal and expert views; consultation with Finance, Law and other departments; financial implications; implementation schedule and milestones; risks and limits; approval sought. Appendices, then a one-page press brief.

## E. Government Order draft (Telangana layout read in two Finance GOs; Health GO not read)

Read: a 2024 Finance GO and a 2016 Finance GO on filling health posts. The Health, Medical and Family Welfare GO of 2021 was a scan and was not read. Label the draft "check against a recent GO of this department".

Order: priority tag if any; "Government of Telangana"; ABSTRACT line (the subject sentence, ending "Issued"); department, GO number and date; "Read the following" (names the department's proposal and earlier GOs); "ORDER:" with numbered paragraphs, one decision each; a table by Head of Department with totals where posts or money are listed; for new schemes, quote the number and date of the sanctioning proceedings; "By order and in the name of the Governor of Telangana"; signing officer and title; To and Copy to lists. Length 1 to 3 pages plus annexes. Financial concurrence is stated in the order text. Sanction amount, budget head and who draws and disburses come from the user only.

## F. NHM PIP input note

Source: NHM PIP guidance note FY2026-27, read in full. The PIP is a one-year request to MoHFW.

Order: activity list by FMR code and Sl. No, quantity, unit cost, amount; key deliverable targets; revalidation list of earlier approvals; justification for items outside cost norms; envelope check (proposal within 25 to 30 percent above the Resource Envelope; check current figures in the guidance note); unit-cost source label for each cost (RoP norm, trade price, or assumption).

## G. District Health Action Plan section

Source: NRHM framework chapter 10, about 2006 to 2007. No current template found. NHM guidance adds a duty to consult local MPs, MLAs and PRIs.

Order: background; situation analysis (coverage, equity, underserved groups, quality); process and consultations; objectives and strategy matrix; workplan (who, when); monitoring and evaluation; budget with unit costs and source of funds, showing costed and uncosted activities; annexures.

## H. HTA-style evidence brief (only for a technology or package choice)

Source: HTAIn Policy Brief Book Vol 2 (27 briefs, about 3 to 4 pages each, read) and the Indian Reference Case paper (read). This is not the formal HTAIn submission. A real submission goes through the HTAIn portal and its Process Manual, which was not opened.

Order: title and issuing unit; Recommendations box (one per policy question); Summary (question, how assessed, headline numbers: extra cost, QALYs gained, ICER); small "About" box with definitions; methods (comparator, perspective, cost source, outcome, discount rate); background (burden); key findings; policy implications; budget impact for the state as a share of the state health budget; conclusion; references.

Reference case points: population, intervention, comparator, outcome; abridged societal view shown beside the payer view; costs from the National Health System Cost Database; QALY preferred; 3% discount, varied 0 to 5%; costs in INR first, USD in brackets with the rate stated; give the reason for any departure; no official Indian cost-effectiveness threshold (the paper allows one times GDP per capita as a stopgap). Reporting checklist named in the paper is HTA-QAC, not CHEERS.

## I. Institute policy brief (ICMR style)

Source: three ICMR institute briefs (cholera vaccine, fluorosis, tuberculosis case finding), read. They credit a 2017 workshop template. No official ICMR template was found. Do not call this one.

Order: title, date, authors and unit; executive summary under one page (problem, finding, 3 to 6 short recommendations, cost of doing nothing); background and context; aim; gaps; findings; recommendations split into short-term and long-term, with one cost line each where known; 8 to 12 references; acknowledgements. Length 4 to 5 pages. Costing is light: one or two unit costs or a cost comparison.

## J. National policy paper and state factsheet (NITI Aayog style)

Source: NITI Health Index 2018 round and the Missing Middle paper, read.

Policy paper: executive summary of about 3 pages that ends in numbered recommendations; problem sized with national numbers; options and international examples; implementation pathway with the role of each level of government; sources footnoted with date. Prices as rupees per family per year with the source and date.

State or district factsheet (Health Index pattern): one page per state or district with base-year and reference-year values side by side, a colour category from most improved to most deteriorated, and a data source and year on every table.

## Front matter for briefs and dossiers

Author line is the institution. State funder, conflicts of interest and reviewers. Name the AI audit agent if one was used. Give a version and date.
